## 📌 About the Project

This is my personal portfolio website, developed to establish my online presence and showcase my work, interests, and development journey.

The website provides visitors with an overview of who I am, what I create, and how they can connect with me.

## ✨ Features

- **Dashboard** – A welcoming homepage with an overview of the website.
- **About Me** – Information about my background, interests, and journey.
- **Projects** – A collection of my development projects and creative work.
- **Photo Gallery** – A space to showcase memorable moments and photographs.
- **Contact** – A dedicated section for communication and collaboration.
- **Responsive Design** – Designed to provide a user-friendly browsing experience.
